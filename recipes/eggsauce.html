Eggsauce Recipe

This is an eggsauce. It is one of my favorite meals and is also one of the most versatile
dishes. It can be eaten with yam, bread, rice etc.

Ingredients

    Eggs
    Onions
    Tomatoes
    Fresh Peppers
    Salt
    Vegetable oil

Steps to Prepare

    Break the eggs, chop the onions, chop the tomatoes, and ground the pepper
    set the pan on fire and pour some oil in
    Put the tomatoes and allow for it to begin frying--Water dries up
    Put the chop onions, pepper and salt
    Put the eggs
    Stir and fry for about 5mins
